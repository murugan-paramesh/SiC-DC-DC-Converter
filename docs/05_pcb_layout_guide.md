# 05 - PCB layout

Board: `hardware/kicad/SiC_PSFB_3kW.kicad_pcb`, 260 x 200 mm, 4 layers, 2 oz copper on all
layers, 2.0 mm FR4. Generated by `tools/kicad/gen_pcb.py` (board configuration) on top of the reusable
`tools/kicad/boardgen.py` builder; the signal tracks come from the negotiated-congestion grid
router in `tools/kicad/router.py` (0.25 mm grid, F.Cu/B.Cu, fan-out stubs and via-free corridors
at the fine-pitch ICs) and the result is checked with KiCad's DRC (`hardware/kicad/drc_report.txt`,
summary in `drc_summary.json`).

## Stack-up and layer usage

| Layer | Primary half (x < 125.5 mm) | Secondary half (x > 134.5 mm) |
|---|---|---|
| F.Cu | components, LR_N / PRI_1 / PRI_2 pours, gate drive, PGND fill | SEC_A / SEC_CT / SEC_B / VOUT / OUT_N pours, control, SGND fill |
| In1.Cu | PGND plane | SGND plane |
| In2.Cu | VBUS plane | SGND plane (x < 186), VOUT plane (x > 186) |
| B.Cu | SW_A / SW_B pours, signals, PGND fill | SEC_A / SEC_B / VOUT pours, signals, SGND fill |

The plane pair VBUS/PGND (In2/In1, 0.2 mm apart) forms a low-inductance DC-link; the bridge
drain/source pins connect straight into the planes through their through-hole pads, so the
high di/dt loop (C5/C6 -> Q -> planes) is tiny.

## Isolation barrier

* No copper on any layer in the band x = 125.5 - 134.5 mm (rule area on all four layers),
  giving 9 mm clearance/creepage for the primary-to-secondary pairs, verified by the DRC rules
  `isolation_primary_secondary` (8 mm) in `SiC_PSFB_3kW.kicad_dru`.
* Components that bridge the barrier: T1 (44 mm between pin rows), CT1 (28 mm, one-turn
  insulated lead), U2/U3 (SOIC-16W, pads spread to 10.2 mm), CY1/CY2 (12.5 mm pitch), PS1-PS4.
* The SIP-7 bias modules only give 5.9 mm between their input and output pins, so a 1.6 x 8 mm
  slot is milled under each module (Edge.Cuts) and the rule `isolation_gate_bias` enforces
  5.5 mm; the slot raises the creepage to > 12 mm. Conformal coating is recommended for the
  primary side (0.5 mm clearance between the 400 V bridge pins and their gate pins is inherent
  to the TO-247-4 package).

## Power paths

* **Primary loop**: VBUS/PGND planes -> Q drains/sources -> SW_A/SW_B pours on B.Cu (3.5 mm
  wide strips, 10 A rms) -> L1 -> LR_N pour (F.Cu) -> C7/C8 -> PRI_1 -> CT1 -> PRI_2 -> T1.
  Clamp diodes D1/D2 sit on the LR_N pour with their VBUS/PGND pins straight into the planes.
* **Secondary**: SEC_A and SEC_B are 38 mm wide pours on both outer layers from the T1 pins
  to the SR drains (41 A rms); SEC_CT runs to the L2 input; VOUT is a 36 mm wide pour on F.Cu,
  B.Cu and the In2 plane, stitched with the electrolytic and MLCC pads; the return current
  flows in the SGND planes through the shunt R3 to the OUT_N terminal.
* Gate loops: each SiC MOSFET's gate network and bias capacitors sit directly below its pins
  (y = 22 - 29 mm), referenced to the Kelvin-source pin; the driver outputs run as 0.5 mm
  tracks.

## Thermal

* Q1-Q4 and D1/D2: vertical TO-247/TO-220 along the top edge, insulated pads to an extruded
  heatsink bar (Rth ~0.8 K/W per device required, ~50 W total).
* Q5-Q8: vertical TO-220 on the top/bottom edges of the secondary half, second heatsink bar
  (~30 W).
* T1, L1, L2: open-frame, 2 m/s airflow lengthwise; transformer loss 9 W, output inductor 6 W.

## Design rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum track / clearance (signals) | 0.2 mm / 0.2 mm |
| PRI_GATE nets | 0.5 mm tracks, 0.3 mm clearance |
| PRI_POWER to PRI_POWER | 1.0 mm |
| PRI_POWER to PRI_GATE | 0.5 mm |
| SEC_POWER to anything | 0.5 mm |
| Primary to secondary | 8.0 mm (5.5 mm + slot at the bias modules) |
| Via | 0.8 / 0.4 mm signals, 1.2 / 0.6 mm power stitching |
| Copper to edge | 0.5 mm |

## What to review before fabrication

1. Confirm the transformer / inductor bobbin pin maps against the parts actually wound (the
   footprints are generic 2 x 8 pin ETD59 and 2 x 2 pin ETD39 patterns).
2. Confirm the heatsink bar geometry: the TO-247 tabs face the board edge at y = 8 mm.
3. Re-run `python3 -m tools.kicad.gen_pcb` after any edit in `design.py`; the DRC summary is
   printed at the end and written to `hardware/kicad/drc_report.txt`.
