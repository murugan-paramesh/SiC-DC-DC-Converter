# 17.2 - Manufacturing, assembly and mechanical notes

Status: **preliminary** - the outputs in `outputs/` are generated by `export_outputs.py` from the
routed board; the DRC state is recorded in `18_Verification/`. Do not release to fabrication before the
independent review of 15_Safety_Reliability/01 and the open items listed there.

## Outputs (generated)
* `outputs/gerbers/` Gerber X2 for all copper, mask, paste, silk, edge; `outputs/drill/` Excellon PTH/NPTH; `outputs/*.pos` pick-and-place (SMD, top); `outputs/*-F_Fab.pdf` assembly drawing; `outputs/*-dimensions.pdf` fabrication drawing (Edge.Cuts + dimensions).
* BOM in `16_BOM/`.

## Assembly
1. SMD reflow (top only). 2. Selective/hand soldering of THT: film capacitors, SIP modules, TO-247 devices (after mounting on the heatsink bars with 1 K/W silicone pads and M3 clips, 1 N m), studs (press-fit M6, then solder). 3. Conformal coating (acrylic, 50 um) over the HV and LV regions; mask the stud terminals and the heatsink NTC pads.
4. Install the CT toroid: pass the SW_A lead (TIW, 4 kV) from J3 through the toroid to J9; secondary wires to the CT pads.
5. Mount the magnetics module (transformer + inductor) on the chassis; connect with 10 mm2 cables, M6 torque 8 N m, keep the J3-L1-T1 loop short (< 300 mm, twisted pair).

## Mechanical interface
Board 340 x 250 mm, 6 x M4 holes (7/333 x 7/243 mm and 170 x 7/243 mm) on 8 mm standoffs. Heatsink bar A (HV bridge) along the top edge x = 20-110 mm, bar B (LV bridge) x = 236-326 mm, both 150 x 60 x 40 mm extrusions bonded to PE. LaunchPad plugs into J10/J11 (BoosterPack pitch to be verified against SPRUII7). Cable entries: HV and LV studs on the left/right edges, control connectors on the bottom edge.

## Electrical test plan (production)
Netlist/continuity test of the bare board; after assembly: 24 V aux current (< 300 mA idle), rail voltages (12/5/3.3 V, 8 x +15/-3 V), PROT_DIS logic test with injected I_L/V signals, hipot 2.5 kVrms HV-LV, 4 kVrms HV-CTRL / 3 kVrms LV-CTRL (1 s routine), then low-voltage functional test (20_Experimental_Test_Plan).
