## 1. Plant and controller structure

Averaged model (03, eq. 10-12): the DAB is a current source into the LV link. The controller output is a **power command** and the modulator inverse (`phi_from_power`, current form) linearises it, so the loop gain does not depend on V1 or on the operating phase shift (without the feedforward K_phi varies from 26.6 to 36.6 A/rad between 650 and 850 V, a +/-15 % gain change that the PI would otherwise have to tolerate). The one-period computation delay plus the PWM update give T_d = 1.5 T_s = 15 us.

The 600 Hz crossover (f_s/167) keeps the delay phase lag at 3 deg and gives a 2.8 % dip for a 50 % load step in the switched simulation (05_Simulation), against the 3 % requirement EL-10. Quantisation: the C2000 EPWM has a 10 ns resolution (1/1000 of the period, 0.36 deg); the ADC is 12 bit (0.25 V on an 850 V range), both negligible against the 1 % regulation requirement.

EPS voltage matching (D of the higher-voltage bridge = d) removes most of the circulating power at d far from 1 and keeps ZVS at light load; it is the firmware default because it needs no table. TPS gives the lowest rms current but the gain over EPS is small at this ratio range; it is kept as an optional look-up table (12_Optimization). All modulation changes are applied by the ISR at the period boundary with the same feedforward, so the voltage loop sees the same linearised plant.

## 4. Model predictive control (research alternative)

An explicit one-step MPC with a disturbance observer on the averaged model gives a 0.1 V dip versus 1.5 V for the PI on a 50 % step; settling (1 V band) 0.0 ms vs 1.28 ms. The explicit form costs ~30 multiply-adds per ISR (equivalent to the PI), but it relies on the load estimate and the plant parameters (C2, delay); in the switched simulation the PI with feedforward already meets EL-08/EL-10, so the MPC remains a documented research option, not the baseline.

## 5. Reverse power flow

In CP mode the power reference is signed; the feedforward returns a negative phi and the same PI trims the regulated port. The switched simulation (05) ramps +6.6 kW to -6.6 kW in 6.6 ms (2 kW/ms slew) with < 0.7 % excursion on either link.
