_Generated by `emi_analysis.py`; analytical pre-estimate, not a measurement._

## 1. Applicable standards (installation: industrial cabinet, DC ports)
* Emission: CISPR 11 / EN 55011 class A group 1 (industrial), conducted 150 kHz - 30 MHz on the DC ports (measured with a DC LISN per CISPR 16-1-2), radiated 30 MHz - 1 GHz at 10 m; IEC 61800-3 category C3 by analogy for a converter inside a drive-like system.
* Immunity: IEC 61000-6-2 (ESD 4/8 kV, RF 10 V/m, EFT 2 kV on power ports, surge 1 kV/2 kV, conducted RF 10 V).
* Compliance is demonstrated only by test in an accredited lab (20_Experimental_Test_Plan, EMI pre-compliance).

## 2. Noise sources
* Differential mode: the HV-bridge input current is pulsating at 2 fs with 4.2 A rms; the link capacitors (4 x 20 uF, ESR 4 mOhm, ESL 20 nH) absorb it but the residual voltage across a 100 Ohm LISN reaches 106 dBuV at 800 kHz without a filter.
* Common mode: four switching nodes with ~20 V/ns edges drive 2.0 A peak pulses through ~100 pF per node to the earthed heatsink; the transformer interwinding capacitance (631 pF) couples HV-side CM noise to the LV port. Envelope estimate: 124 dBuV at 150 kHz.

## 3. Required attenuation and filter proposal
Required (with 6 dB margin): DM 39 dB, CM 57 dB in the 150 kHz - 2 MHz range.

| stage | DM | CM |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 uH (powder core) + 2.2 uF X2 (1000 V film) | 3 mH nanocrystalline CM choke (20 A) + 2 x 4.7 nF Y1 to PE |
| 2 | 10 uH + 2.2 uF | 3 mH + 2 x 4.7 nF |

Calculated insertion loss at 150 kHz: DM 52 dB, CM 56 dB. The Y-capacitor leakage current at 100 kHz is driven by the converter, not the mains, so the touch-current limit does not apply to the DC ports, but the PE current must be bonded (SAF-05).

## 4. Layout and construction measures (implemented in 08_KiCad_PCB)
* Switching loops: each half-bridge leg has its 2220 MLCC decoupling within 10 mm of the drain/source pins; the DC-link film capacitors feed the planes.
* The transformer and inductor are the only components crossing the HV/LV barrier; a shield foil (connected to the HV link midpoint) between primary and secondary is specified as an option in 06_Magnetics to reduce C_iw coupling.
* Gate loops are referenced to the Kelvin source; the drivers sit within 15 mm of the gates; the isolated driver bias modules have < 10 pF isolation capacitance each (to limit dv/dt current into the control ground).
* Control ground is a single plane under the DSP, connected to PE at one point (star) through the mounting hole near the connector; sensing uses isolated amplifiers (AMC1311) so no HV reference enters the control ground.
* Heatsinks bonded to PE with short straps; Y capacitors from each DC rail to PE at the port connectors (the first CM filter element).

## 5. Pre-compliance test plan (summary, details in 20)
1. Conducted emissions on both DC ports with DC LISNs, 150 kHz - 30 MHz, QP and AV, at 25/50/100 % load, both power directions.
2. Radiated emissions pre-scan 30 MHz - 1 GHz with a near-field probe set and a 3 m semi-anechoic scan if available.
3. Immunity: ESD on connectors and enclosure, EFT on DC ports and CAN, surge on DC ports, conducted RF on cables - functional criterion: no fault trip, < 1 % regulation disturbance.
