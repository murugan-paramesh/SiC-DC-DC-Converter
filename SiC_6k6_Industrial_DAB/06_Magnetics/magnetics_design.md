Selection: **E65/32/27 pair, 3C95** - lowest total loss with a feasible fill factor and 14 K rise; E55 does not fit the window, E70/PQ65 add volume without benefit. Planar alternative: a planar E64 with 4-layer PCB windings would need 28 turns of 2 mm2 (two 70 um layers in parallel per turn ... > 24 layers) - not practical at 800 V / 28 turns; planar construction is attractive only for n ~ 1 low-turn-count designs, so the conventional litz/bobbin construction is retained.

## 3. Integrated leakage option

The non-interleaved winding gives 16 uH (26 % of the 64 uH needed) with 1.16x the copper loss. A deliberate leakage layer (3 mm spacer between P and S) would reach ~40 uH but the tolerance on leakage of a hand-wound bobbin transformer is +/-20 %, which maps directly into +/-20 % power capability (eq. 4). The baseline keeps the leakage small and uses the discrete gapped inductor whose value is set by the gap (+/-5 %).

## 5. Insulation system (HV-LV basic, 850 V working, see 15_Safety_Reliability)

* Bobbin: glass-filled PBT/PET, CTI >= 400, 6 mm flanges; primary and secondary on separate bobbin halves is NOT used (interleaving needs a single bobbin), so the
  interwinding insulation is 3 layers of 50 um polyimide tape (each layer rated > 5 kV) plus 6 mm margin tape at both ends: creepage along the bobbin > 12 mm.
* Lead-outs: triple-insulated wire (TIW) for the primary exits, secondary litz with 0.4 mm PFA sleeve.
* High-frequency stress: the 100 kHz square voltage (dv/dt ~ 20 V/ns at the edges) drives displacement current through C_iw (~0.6 nF): i = C dv/dt ~ 12 A peak pulses - the
  Y-capacitors and the EMI filter (14) return this current; partial-discharge inception is not expected below 1 kV peak across 150 um polyimide but must be tested.
* Core bonded to the heatsink/chassis (PE) through a thermal pad; the core is at protective-earth potential, so the primary-to-core and secondary-to-core insulation is also basic (850 V / 450 V).

## 6. Test procedure (before assembly on the PCB)

1. Turns ratio and polarity (1 kHz, 1 V): N1/N2 = 2.00 +/- 1 %.
2. Lm at 100 kHz, 1 V: expect 10 mH class (ungapped 3C95), record; leakage with the secondary shorted: expect < 6 uH.
3. Winding resistance (4-wire): compare with the calculated DC values (R1 = 41.1 mOhm, R2 = 10.3 mOhm).
4. Interwinding capacitance at 100 kHz.
5. Hipot: 2.5 kVrms 60 s primary-secondary and 2.5 kVrms primary-core / secondary-core (basic, 850 V working); partial discharge screening at 1.5 x working voltage if the lab has the equipment.
6. Thermal run at rated current (back-to-back test with a second unit or on the converter at reduced voltage) - record hot-spot with a thermocouple under the outer layer.
7. Inductor: L at 0, 10, 20 A DC bias (must stay within -10 % at 24 A), gap measurement, hipot 1.5 kVrms winding-core.
